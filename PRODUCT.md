# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing React 19 + Vite application with Tailwind CSS and Lucide icons. Preserve the current stack and section structure unless the user explicitly changes that constraint.

## Users

The primary audience is a broad mix of recruiters, potential clients, and collaborators evaluating Rifqi Adrianto's skills, work, experience, and contact details.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio makes it possible for visitors to quickly understand Rifqi's capabilities and contact him for opportunities. Success means a visitor can scan the portfolio, find credible evidence of skills and experience, review selected projects, and reach out without friction.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines full-stack web development work with evidence of project leadership, organizational involvement, certifications, and machine-learning-related work. Its strongest truthful position is a practical, growing developer who can contribute both technical implementation and structured project leadership.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single scrolling portfolio containing navigation, an introductory hero, about and capability summaries, skills, projects, experience/certification evidence, and contact information. The site supports light and dark themes and responsive desktop/mobile navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current React/Vite stack, existing section structure, and real project content.
- Keep the portfolio's navigation anchors for Home, About, Skills, Projects, Experience, and Contact.
- Present real links for LinkedIn, CV materials, email, phone, and project destinations where available in the source.
- Support responsive web layouts and the existing theme toggle.
- Do not invent testimonials, clients, employment claims, benchmarks, awards, project outcomes, or other proof not present in the repository.

## Brand Commitments

- The identity is Rifqi Adrianto / Rifqi Portfolio.
- The existing voice presents Rifqi as an undergraduate informatics student and developing full-stack web developer.
- Preserve the factual project, experience, certification, and contact content already represented in the source.

## Evidence on Hand

- Introductory positioning and CTA: `src/components/HeroSection.jsx`
- About, capabilities, LinkedIn, and CV link: `src/components/AboutSection.jsx`
- Skills and project content: `src/components/SkillsSection.jsx` and `src/components/ProjectsSection.jsx`
- Certifications, organizational experiences, and supporting images: `src/components/ExperienceSection.jsx` and `public/experience/`
- Contact channels: `src/components/ContactSection.jsx`
- Site navigation and responsive behavior: `src/components/Navbar.jsx`

## Product Principles

- Make credibility scannable before asking visitors to explore deeply.
- Lead with real work and evidence rather than generic claims.
- Make contacting Rifqi an obvious, low-friction next step.
- Preserve a clear path across capabilities, projects, experience, and contact.
- Keep the experience usable and legible across desktop and mobile.
